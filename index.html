About Timber Kennel Co.

Welcome to Timber Kennel Co, a family-run basset hound breeding program in Texas. Our kennel is home to Timber, our loyal and loving sire, and our two sweet girls, Lucy and Ellie.

Our puppies are raised in a warm, kid-friendly environment, making them well-socialized and ready to become part of your family.
